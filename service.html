Frequently Asked Questions

Common Tailoring Queries

Can I bring my own fabric?

Yes, absolutely! Most of our customers bring their favorite fabric from their chosen textile store. We inspect the cloth, recommend suitable styles, and tailor it to your exact measurements.

How long does stitching take?

Standard turnaround for shirts and trousers is 2 to 3 days. Suits and blazers take 5 to 7 days including trial fitting. If you have an urgent function or wedding, express stitching is available on request.

What if the garment needs a minor adjustment after trial?

We provide a 100% Fit Guarantee. During your trial, if any seam, waist, or length requires fine-tuning, our master tailor adjusts it free of charge on the spot.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all UPI payments (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM), debit/credit cards, and cash.
